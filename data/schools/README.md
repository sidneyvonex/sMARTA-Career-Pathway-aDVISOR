# Verified School Dataset

This directory will contain versioned school records for the pilot counties once
the authoritative county list and individual records have been verified.

No school record should be added as authoritative without:

- official school code;
- school name and location;
- cluster;
- pathways and subject combinations actually offered;
- accommodation, gender and SNE information where officially available;
- at least one authoritative source;
- the date checked;
- an explicit verification status.

Conflicting or incomplete records must remain hidden from authoritative product
flows or be clearly labelled `UNVERIFIED`.
