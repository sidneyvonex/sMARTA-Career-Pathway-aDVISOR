# Smarta Shauri 17-Page User Manual Revision Design

**Date:** 4 August 2026  
**Status:** Approved  
**Deliverable:** Editable Microsoft Word document (`.docx`)

## Purpose

Revise the existing large-image Smarta Shauri user manual so that it includes a project description and the three approved project objectives, preserves readable screenshots, and adds the institution and programme workflow that is not fully visible in the current Education Goals figure.

## Approved project description

Smarta Shauri is a Progressive Web Application that provides CBC-aligned career guidance to Grade 9 and Grade 10-12 learners in Central Kenya. It combines academic evidence, a RIASEC-based assessment, pathway and subject-combination exploration, institution and programme research, and role-based support from parents, counsellors, school administrators, and system administrators.

## Approved objectives

1. Build a Progressive Web Application providing CBC-aligned career pathway guidance to Grade 9 and Grade 10-12 students in Central Kenya.
2. Design a RIASEC-based assessment evaluating student interests, competencies, and personality to recommend suitable CBC Senior Secondary pathways.
3. Build a searchable database covering Senior Schools, universities and TVET campuses in Central Kenya with CBC programmes and entry requirements.

## Page structure

The revised manual will contain exactly 17 pages:

1. Formal university cover page using the already approved student, course, instructor, supervisor, and submission details.
2. Project description and three numbered objectives in a branded, readable layout.
3. Public introduction: Home, About, and Pathways.
4. Public guidance and registration: Register, How It Works, and For Schools.
5. Account access: Login, Forgot Password, and Reset Password.
6. Account completion and learner start: Verify Email, Accept Invitation, and Student Dashboard.
7. Student navigation and profile: My Profile, mobile navigation, and mobile More menu.
8. Academic evidence: My Grades, My Progress, and the Education Goals introduction.
9. Institution discovery: institution search, Central Kenya location/type filters, and institution selection.
10. Programme discovery: programme search, programme detail, entry requirements, and saving an education goal.
11. RIASEC assessment and alerts: Career Quiz, Career Profile, and Notifications.
12. Choices and planning: Explore Choices, Compare Choices, and My Plan.
13. Parent experience: Parent Access, Parent Dashboard, and Learner Summary.
14. Counsellor experience: Counsellor Dashboard, My Students, and Learner Detail.
15. Counsellor and school administration: Counsellor Notes, School Dashboard, and Learners.
16. School configuration: Counsellors, Offerings, and School Profile.
17. System administration and recovery: the five system-administrator screens and Page Not Found, arranged as readable paired figures where necessary.

## Screenshot and annotation rules

- Retain the current large-image visual treatment and Smarta Shauri green/gold brand system.
- Use actual implemented interface states; do not invent pages or claim unsupported data coverage.
- Add focused screenshots of the Education Goals page after an institution is selected and after a programme is selected.
- Show the search, filter, selection, programme-detail, entry-requirement, and save-goal controls using numbered arrows or callouts.
- Give every screenshot a one-line explanation, a navigation path, and a short action label.
- Exclude credentials, authentication tokens, private notes, and unnecessary personal information.
- Use three large figures per page by default; page 17 may use a denser but still legible grid to preserve the approved 17-page total.
