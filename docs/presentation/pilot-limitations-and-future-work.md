# Known limitations and future work

> **Audit status (2026-07-31):** These are previously documented pilot
> limitations. The audit must confirm that the application and presentation
> disclose them consistently and identify any additional limitations.

Use this content as the final presentation slide. The limitations are deliberate
boundaries of the pilot, not hidden claims.

## Current pilot limitations

- Five counties only: Kiambu, Murang'a, Nyeri, Kirinyaga and Nyandarua.
- Curated demonstration schools and offerings; no live Ministry or school MIS feed.
- Interest alignment is based on one RIASEC instrument and is advisory, not predictive.
- Academic evidence is entered or verified in the system; national assessment import is
  outside this pilot.
- Parent access requires learner approval, but production identity verification and
  consent recovery need further policy work.
- Notifications are in-app demonstration records; scheduled SMS/email delivery is not
  part of the final-project scope.
- Offline support covers the application shell, public framework and expiring assessment
  drafts. Private dashboards and submissions still require a network.
- Pilot analytics describe recorded activity; they do not establish educational impact.
- Accessibility has automated and sampled browser coverage; formal testing with disabled
  learners and assistive-technology users remains future research.

## Recommended next phase

1. Conduct moderated usability tests with learners, counsellors, parents and school staff
   in all five counties.
2. Add formal consent, retention, deletion and safeguarding policies before real learner
   data is collected.
3. Validate framework and school-offering imports with the responsible education
   authorities and participating schools.
4. Add secure roster/grade imports, conflict review and provenance reconciliation.
5. Evaluate the interest instrument and explanation language for Kenyan context and
   language accessibility.
6. Add delivery-backed reminders, appointment workflows and operational support SLAs.
7. Complete WCAG 2.2 AA testing with screen readers, keyboard users, zoom and low-bandwidth
   devices.
8. Run a measured pilot before considering more counties; use evidence, not feature count,
   to decide expansion.

## Closing statement

> Smart Ashauri helps learners make a more informed, explainable and supported provisional
> choice. People remain responsible for the decision, and official policy remains the
> authority.
